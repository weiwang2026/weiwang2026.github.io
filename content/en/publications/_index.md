title: Publications
---

Professor Wei Wang has established an outstanding research record spanning organic synthesis, chemical biology, and drug discovery. He has published over 360 scientific papers in prestigious journals, including *Nature Catalysis*, *Nature Cell Biology*, *Nature Communications*, *Chem*, *JACS*, and *Angewandte Chemie*, and others. His research has also resulted in 18 granted patents and the establishment of three biotechnology and drug discovery companies, demonstrating both significant scientific impact and a strong commitment to translating fundamental discoveries into therapeutic innovations.
