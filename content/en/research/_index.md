---
title: "Research"
research_intro: >-
  We develop innovative chemical tools at the intersection of synthetic chemistry,
  chemical biology, and drug discovery. By integrating molecular design, selective
  chemical transformations, and biological investigation, we seek new ways to
  understand and modulate biological systems and advance targeted therapeutics.
research_areas:
  - id: "synthetic-chemistry"
    title: "Synthetic Chemistry"
    image: "images/research/synthetic-chemistry-20261108-v2.webp"
    image_fallback: "images/research/synthetic-chemistry-20261108.webp"
    image_alt: "Scientific illustration of asymmetric catalysis, photocatalysis, and medicinal chemistry"
    overview: >-
      We develop efficient and selective synthetic methodologies for constructing
      structurally complex and biologically relevant molecules. Integrating catalysis,
      photochemistry, and molecular design, our work expands the chemical tools
      available for biological research and drug discovery.
    focuses:
      - title: "Asymmetric Catalysis"
        desc: >-
          Development of stereoselective catalytic methodologies, including
          asymmetric cascade reactions, for the efficient construction of
          chiral molecules and pharmaceutically relevant scaffolds.
      - title: "Photocatalysis"
        desc: >-
          Investigation of light-driven catalytic transformations to achieve
          selective bond formation and molecular functionalization through
          controlled photochemical processes.
      - title: "Medicinal Chemistry"
        desc: >-
          Design and synthesis of bioactive small molecules and drug-like scaffolds,
          integrating medicinal chemistry principles and structure–activity
          relationship studies to guide molecular optimization.

  - id: "chemical-biology"
    title: "Chemical Biology"
    image: "images/research/chemical-biology-20261108.webp"
    image_fallback: "images/research/chemical-biology-20261108.webp"
    image_alt: "Scientific illustration of bioorthogonal labeling, proximity chemistry, and biomolecular engineering"
    overview: >-
      We develop chemical tools to visualize, investigate, and regulate biomolecules
      in complex biological systems. Our research combines selective bioorthogonal
      reactivity, proximity-enabled methods, and biomolecular functionalization
      to investigate protein function and cellular processes.
    focuses:
      - title: "Bioorthogonal Chemistry"
        desc: >-
          Development of selective chemical reactions for molecular labeling,
          imaging, and controlled activation in living systems, enabling
          the investigation and manipulation of biological processes.
      - title: "Proximity Chemistry"
        desc: >-
          Development of proximity-enabled chemical strategies for the selective
          modification and functional regulation of proteins, with applications
          in understanding protein interactions and cellular signaling.
      - title: "Biomolecular Engineering"
        desc: >-
          Development of chemical approaches for the functionalization of peptides,
          proteins, and glycoconjugates, including bioconjugation technologies
          for antibody–drug conjugates and other biomolecular therapeutics.

  - id: "drug-discovery"
    title: "Drug Discovery"
    image: "images/research/drug-discovery-20261108-v2.webp"
    image_fallback: "images/research/drug-discovery-20261108.webp"
    image_alt: "Scientific illustration of targeted protein degradation, precision therapeutics, and AI-assisted drug discovery"
    overview: >-
      We integrate medicinal chemistry, chemical biology, and computational
      approaches to develop new therapeutic strategies. Our research focuses
      on targeted protein modulation, selective drug activation, and
      AI-assisted molecular design for precision medicine.
    focuses:
      - title: "Targeted Protein Degradation"
        desc: >-
          Development of targeted protein degradation strategies, including
          PROTACs, molecular glues, and conjugate-based degraders, to selectively
          eliminate disease-associated proteins.
      - title: "Precision Therapeutics"
        desc: >-
          Design of selectively activated therapeutic agents, including
          bioorthogonal prodrugs and senolytic compounds, for applications
          in cancer, neurodegenerative diseases, and age-related disorders.
      - title: "AI-Assisted Drug Discovery"
        desc: >-
          Integration of artificial intelligence, molecular modeling, and
          experimental evaluation to support rational drug design,
          molecular optimization, and the discovery of new therapeutic candidates.
---
