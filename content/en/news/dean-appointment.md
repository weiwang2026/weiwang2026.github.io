Professor Wei Wang has been appointed **Dean of the School of Chemistry and Chemical Biology** at Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo. This appointment marks an important milestone in his academic career and in the development of the school's interdisciplinary research and education.

As Chair Professor and Dean, Professor Wang brings experience spanning organic synthesis, chemical biology, and drug discovery. The Wang Lab continues to pursue research that connects chemistry, biology, and artificial intelligence to develop new molecular tools and therapeutic strategies.
