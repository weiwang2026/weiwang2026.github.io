The **Wang Group was established at Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo, in January 2026**, opening a new chapter in interdisciplinary chemical and biomedical research.

Led by Professor Wei Wang, the group brings together researchers working across **precision organic synthesis, chemical biology, and AI-driven drug discovery**. Its research focuses on developing molecular tools and therapeutic approaches for challenging problems in cancer, neurodegenerative diseases, and aging.

The establishment of the group creates a collaborative setting in which ideas from chemistry, biology, and artificial intelligence can inform the design and discovery of next-generation therapeutics.
