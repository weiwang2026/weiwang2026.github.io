Professor Wei Wang was inducted into the **2025 Class of the College of Fellows of the American Institute for Medical and Biological Engineering (AIMBE)**.

Announced on March 31, 2025, the distinction recognizes his contributions to synthetic biology technologies and to therapeutic and imaging tools for neurodegenerative diseases, cancer, and infectious diseases.

Election to the AIMBE College of Fellows is a distinguished recognition within medical and biological engineering, highlighting the impact of research that bridges engineering, medicine, and related disciplines.
