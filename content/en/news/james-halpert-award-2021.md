Professor Wei Wang was named the **2021 recipient of the Dr. James Halpert Faculty Research Award** by the University of Arizona College of Pharmacy.

The award recognized his research contributions at the intersection of advanced chemical synthesis, chemical biology, and drug discovery. The college highlighted his work on potential therapeutics and molecular diagnostic probes, as well as his scientific publications and contributions to the research community.

The announcement was published in December 2021 as part of the college's faculty and staff awards.

**Source:** [University of Arizona College of Pharmacy — 2021 Faculty and Staff Award Winners](https://www.pharmacy.arizona.edu/news/2021/2021-faculty-and-staff-award-winners).
